THE SYSTEM.

Independent baseball analysis. Not betting-app consensus. A disciplined process before every lock.

01 / STARTERS

Pitching foundation

SIERA, xFIP, ERA, WHIP, strikeouts, walks, workload, matchup, and expected innings.

02 / OFFENSE

Real lineups

Confirmed batting orders, wRC+, platoon splits, injuries, current form, and hitter matchups.

03 / BULLPEN

The final outs

Relief strength, high-leverage options, individual workloads from the last 2–3 days, and postseason form.

04 / CONTEXT

Everything else

Park factors, wind direction, weather, defense, and risk assessment before locking the winner.

Frozen means frozen.

Once an official pick is announced, the team cannot be casually changed. Newly verified material news must be documented before any exceptional switch. This site currently preserves official selection identity once added.

What's automatic today?

The public site automatically displays the current published picks and recalculates the tracker from the data file. It does not automatically generate picks, fetch lineups, or verify results yet; those require a separately configured data source and scheduled analysis.
