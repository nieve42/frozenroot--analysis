Frozen means frozen.

Once an official pick is announced, the team cannot be casually changed. Newly verified material news must be documented before any exceptional switch. This site currently preserves official selection identity once added.

Publish a new official pick

GITHUB MANAGED

Ask Root in ChatGPT to prepare a new MLB pick with a predicted score for each team and a projected total runs. After confirming and locking it, have Root update the website's site.json through the connected GitHub account.

Example forecast (illustrative only): Rays 4 – Guardians 2, projected total 6 runs. The projected score is saved with the official pregame pick and never replaces the actual final result.

Locked teams stay fixed. A change to the public record requires a deliberate source-data edit and a new GitHub commit.

Settle a completed game

VERIFY FIRST

Once a final result is verified, update the corresponding pick's status in site.json to win, loss, push, or void. The win/loss totals recalculate on the site.

GitHub Pages updates the public website after you commit changes to the hosting branch.

NFL score forecasts

When Root locks a football moneyline pick, we can also publish the estimated final points for each team and projected combined total in nfl-picks.json. These are independent pregame forecasts, never automatic final scores.

NBA score forecasts

Root’s official NBA moneyline picks and predicted scores are published separately in nba-picks.json. Each pick can show projected points for both teams and a projected total. Today’s basketball schedule is generated separately and never locks picks automatically.

NHL goal forecasts

NHL winner picks and projected team goals are saved separately in nhl-picks.json. Today's hockey schedule is refreshed independently; it never creates or locks official predictions automatically. Final scores do not overwrite pregame forecasts.

What's automatic today?

For NFL, NBA, and NHL, new schedules trigger independent, automatic pregame winner and team-score predictions. Their score-based model uses verified past game results and is not a live ChatGPT analysis. The published predictions are frozen, and the system checks final scores to settle automatic picks. MLB and Root + owner official locks remain separate. Injuries, lineup changes and bookmaker odds are not inputs to this free model.
